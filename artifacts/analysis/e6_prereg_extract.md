- Implementation: `lam_confirm_e6.py` (CPU, after stage 3) copies the functions verbatim. **Self-test:** from the E4b/E5b files it reproduces the e6_lambda Qwen2.5-0.5B results:
  - U1 removes 0.984 [0.881, 1.054] of the λ = 1 loss.
  - U1 − λ0.7 = −0.86 pp [−1.74, −0.24].
  - M3 AUROC = 0.929 [0.840, 0.986].
  - Exact λ ties go to the smaller λ; this is made explicit by rounding the agreement scores to 12 decimals.
- **U1**: choose λ in the grid that maximises the mean, over the pair's two tasks, of merged-vs-single-adapter prediction agreement (argmax class; Spearman for STS-B) on **unlabeled** calibration inputs.
  - Calibration set: min(200, ⌊n_eval/2⌋) eval examples per task, drawn with `default_rng(20260927 + task index)`, disjoint from the scored examples. Labels are never used.
- Scoring on the eval examples outside calibration: D_test(λ). Reference λ_sel = held-out-selected λ (tuned TA, E4b grid). Regret = 100·(D_test(λ_rule) − D_test(λ_sel)) pp.
- Data: E6 cross-task pairs **R0 + S1 + P** pooled (S2 excluded). **Primary scope: G4 = {0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 1.0}, τ = 0.05.**
  CIs: task-block bootstrap 2,000, weights c_a·c_b, frozen seed rule (20260927 + Σ ord(scope key)).
- **H-U1**: SUPPORTED iff the 95% CI upper bounds of mean[regret(U1) − regret(λ = 0.7)] **and** mean[regret(U1) − regret(λ = 1.0)] are both < 0.
- **H-U1b**: SUPPORTED iff U1 removes ≥ 80% of the avoidable loss of λ = 1, i.e. 1 − mean regret(U1) / mean regret(λ = 1) ≥ 0.80 (point estimate; CI reported).
  **NOT APPLICABLE** if mean regret(λ = 1) ≤ 0, because the ratio is then undefined and there is no avoidable loss. This guard is not in e6_lambda (there, λ = 1 regret was always positive). It was added before any E6 merge, after the smoke test showed a spurious ratio with a non-positive denominator.
- **H-M3**: D̂_U = 1 − max_λ∈G4 agreement(λ); y = 1[D_test(λ_sel) > 0.05]. SUPPORTED iff AUROC ≥ 0.75 **and** CI lower bound > 0.5.
- **H-M3acc** (accuracy): decide "harmful merge" iff D̂_U ≥ **0.1174999999999999**.
  - The threshold is **frozen**: the accuracy-maximising cut on the 780 e6_lambda pairs (in-sample accuracy 0.879, prevalence 0.247), computed before any E6 merge.
  - SUPPORTED iff the CI lower bound of accuracy(M3) − accuracy(always merge) > 0.
  - Sensitivity: τ 0.02 → 0.0504428860721516; τ 0.10 → 0.2375.
- Secondary: per population; G7 (U1 and λ_sel over the 7-point grid); L-cal (labeled calibration) reference; eval-optimal floor; unfitted D̂_U > τ; recall/precision.
  The four hypotheses are reported separately, without multiplicity correction. If R0 or S1 is not completed (cap), the primary scope uses the completed populations, and this is stated.
