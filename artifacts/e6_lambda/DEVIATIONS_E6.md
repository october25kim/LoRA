# E6 deviations and clarifications (appended in time order; RULES.md sha256 199a9cb7…709f0c is unchanged)

- **C1 (2026-09-27 22:32 KST, before any rule was scored).** STS-B is a regression task scored by Spearman correlation, not argmax accuracy. For STS-B, U1 "agreement" is the Spearman correlation between merged and single-adapter predictions on the calibration inputs (still label-free), and test and calibration "accuracy" is the Spearman correlation with the labels, as in the paper's metric. Found when the recomputed metrics failed to match the reported ones.

Added **after** the first full run of `code/evaluate.py` (results had been seen), 2026-09-27 22:39 KST:
- **A1 (post-results).** The "practically useful" reading in RULES.md ("mean regret CI entirely below the better fixed baseline") is made operational as follows. The task-block CI of the paired difference, regret(rule) − regret(better of W1, W2 in that scope), must lie entirely below 0, and the rule must remove ≥ 50% of the better baseline's avoidable loss. The better baseline is chosen per scope from the same data (`code/evaluate_addon.py`, `results/addon_paired_usefulness.csv`).
- **A2 (post-results).** M3 with the pre-declared unfitted threshold (D̂_U > τ) ranked pairs well but was badly calibrated: 1 − agreement overestimates D, and its accuracy fell below always-merge. An added variant, **M3c**, keeps the same label-free score but picks its threshold to maximize accuracy on fit pairs that share no task with the test pair (within-backbone and LOBO). It uses labels of *other* tasks' pairs, like F1–F3 (`results/addon_M3_calibrated.csv`). M3c is exploratory twice over.
- **A3 (bug fix, no change in substance).** The bootstrap seed in `evaluate.py` first used Python's salted `hash()`, which is not reproducible across processes. It was replaced by a deterministic key (sum of character codes). `evaluate.py` and `evaluate_addon.py` now share the same task-block draws, and two consecutive runs gave byte-identical output.
- **Observation (not a deviation).** W3 (norm-preserving λ_np) snaps to 0.7 in almost all pairs, and W4 (projection-preserving λ_pp) snaps to 1.0 in all pairs, so in practice W3 ≈ "fixed λ = 0.7" and W4 ≡ W1. F1 chose 0.7 in every fold. A fixed λ = 0.7 was not itself a pre-declared rule; it enters only through W3 and F1.
