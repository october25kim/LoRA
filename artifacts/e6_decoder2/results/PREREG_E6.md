## 0. Disclosure and purpose
- **Written after these results were known:**
  - E1b (BERT R0: H1 FAIL ρ = −0.194, H2 INCONCLUSIVE); E1c (BERT P: both INCONCLUSIVE); E4a (RoBERTa P: both INCONCLUSIVE, ρ 0.161 / 0.112).
  - **E4b (Qwen2.5-0.5B P, primary)**: ρ(O_A, D) = 0.345, CI [−0.096, 0.609], Holm p = 0.030, LOTO 1.00 → INCONCLUSIVE; ρ(cos, D) = 0.368, CI [−0.079, 0.652], Holm p = 0.024 → INCONCLUSIVE.
    The gate fired in 66/91 P pairs, and the sub-30° overlap sat mostly in `layers.0.self_attn.k_proj`.
  - **E5** (on the E4b adapters): no method beat tuned TA by ≥ 0.5 pp; ρ was unchanged with the λ grid extended to 2.0; D had high sampling reliability.
  - The post hoc exploratory λ-rule analysis **e6_lambda** (box `/workspace/lora-paper/e6_lambda/`, 780 pairs on BERT/RoBERTa/Qwen2.5-0.5B): rule U1, fixed λ = 0.7 and score M3 performed well post hoc (§6c).
- Consequence: E6 is a **replication with every E4b analysis choice frozen** (predictors, rules, thresholds, populations, tests, reading). Nothing is tuned on E6 data.
  The λ-rule hypotheses (§6c) are frozen from e6_lambda before any E6 merge.
- Purpose:
  - Test whether the positive, permutation-significant, sub-threshold E4b decoder association (O_A / task-vector cosine vs TA merge loss) replicates on a larger decoder of the same family.
  - Report where the low-angle overlap sits, and whether the gate helps.
  - Test, confirmatorily, the label-free λ rule U1 and the merge-decision score M3 on a new backbone.

## 1. Adapters (= E4b §1 except the backbone and gradient checkpointing)
- Backbone `Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B` (base) @ `8faed761d45a263340a0528343f099c05c9a4323`, `Qwen2ForSequenceClassification`, last non-pad token pooling.
  The tokenizer is identical to Qwen2.5-0.5B (tokenizer.json sha256 `c0382117…`; pad id 151643, right padding), so inputs, truncation (256) and probe token counts are reused.
  Why not Llama: no HF token is available on the GPU machine (gated). Qwen2.5-1.5B is the ungated, already-downloaded next size.
- LoRA r = 8, α = 16, dropout 0.1 on q/k/v/o/gate/up/down in all 28 layers = **196 LoRA layers** (asserted).
  d_out: q/o/down 1536, k/v 256, gate/up 8960. Head `score` Linear(1536 → num_labels, no bias), fully trained and saved.
- Recipe, budgets (≈ 20.2k steps per seed), splits, held-out 1,000, eval subsets, micro-batch table (32; imdb/yelp 16 × 2), OOM fallback, collapse monitoring: **E4b verbatim**.
- **Deviation D-GC: gradient checkpointing** (HF Trainer, `use_reentrant=False`). It is needed for memory on the 16 GB GPU.
  Throwaway probes without GC: sst2 micro 32 peaked at 14.9 GB; rte micro 16 and imdb micro 8 ran out of memory. With GC every probed task stayed ≤ 11.6 GB.
  GC recomputes activations and does not change the optimisation target; dropout RNG is preserved.
- **Deviation D-SEEDS: seeds [0, 1] fixed**, primary population **P**, as realised in E4b. The E4b projection-dependent seed rule (§1b) is not used; the pilot still computes and records the projection.

## 5. Merges (= E4b §5)
- TA W0 + λ(ΔW1 + ΔW2) on 196 layers, own heads, λ ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 1.0} chosen per pair on the held-out sets (ties → smaller λ). **D = 1 − mean normalized eval score at λ\***.
- TIES-lite and the θ★ = 30° gate as in E4b. Precision: bf16(W0 + λΔ), Δ in fp32; fp32 head; eval batch 128.
- **Memory-only restructuring (D-MEM):** deltas are recomputed per layer on demand with the same operation; merged weights are written layer by layer.
  The fp32 evaluator keeps W0 on CPU, and the TIES top-20% threshold uses the exact CPU order statistic. The arithmetic is identical.
  This was **verified bit-for-bit**: the E6 code, configured for Qwen2.5-0.5B, reproduced the E4b stage-0/1/2 outputs on the E4b adapters (4 tasks, 6 P pairs, all fields, predictions included) before hashing.
- **D-E3: the E3 subset is NOT run.** Unmodified `e3.run()` keeps both all-layer fp32 deltas on the GPU (~10.5 GB) next to the model. E3 was secondary in E4b, and E5a covered those methods.
- **5b. Extended λ (secondary, D-LAMEXT):** after the E4b-grid selection (λ\*, D unchanged), TA is also evaluated at λ ∈ {1.3, 1.5, 2.0} on the held-out and evaluation sets (E5b field names).
  `lam_selected_G7` is recorded. Per-example eval predictions are saved for both single adapters and for the merged model at **every** λ of both grids (`preds/`).

## 6. Analysis
### 6a. Confirmatory primary (= E4b §6 verbatim; `e6_analysis.py` = `e4b_analysis.py` with TAG e6)
- **H1** ρ(O_A, D) > 0; **H2** ρ(tv_cosine, D) > 0 on **P**. 10,000-relabeling one-sided permutation p, Holm over {H1, H2}, task-block bootstrap 2,000, LOTO.
- **PASS** iff ρ ≥ 0.4 ∧ Holm p < 0.05 ∧ ≥ 75% LOTO ρ > 0.2. **FAIL** iff not PASS ∧ bootstrap upper < 0.3. Otherwise **INCONCLUSIVE**.
- Secondary as in E4b (other populations descriptive, pooled, θ_min, gate, null, reliability, S2, fixed-λ ρ, TIES/gate vs TA). There is no E3 contrast.
  Cross-backbone Spearman adds E4b P/R0/S1.
- **Replication reading (descriptive, fixed in advance):**
  - The E4b pattern is *reproduced* for a predictor iff E6 P gives ρ > 0 **and** Holm p < 0.05.
  - PASS: the association clears the pre-registered bar on the larger decoder.
  - FAIL, or ρ ≤ 0: the E4b association does not replicate.
  - INCONCLUSIVE with Holm p ≥ 0.05: not reproduced at this power.
### 6b. Extra (secondary/exploratory; `e6_extra.py`; no decision rule)
- Location of θ_min < 30° pair-layers: by module, layer index and depth quartile; share in `layers.0.self_attn.k_proj`; E4b recomputed for comparison.
- Gate firing, and GATE − TA on gate-active pairs (task-block CI, Wilcoxon, W/T/L).
- **Pooled decoder ρ** (exploratory) = mean of the E4b and E6 ρ, and the difference E6 − E4b. Joint task-block bootstrap (2,000) and joint task-relabeling permutation (10,000) on common valid tasks.
### 6c. Confirmatory SECONDARY: λ rule U1 and merge decision M3 (frozen from e6_lambda; do not enter the H1/H2 verdict)
- Frozen code: `e6_lambda_code_frozen/` is a verbatim copy of `e6_lambda/code/*.py` + `RULES.md`, with sha256 in `SHA256SUMS` and in `prereg_e6.json → lambda_rule_hypotheses`.
  `RULES.md` sha256 = `199a9cb7…709f0c`; `build_unlabeled.py` = `5b0a2124…`; `evaluate.py` = `3b5c5b66…`; `evaluate_addon.py` = `9ed611b6…`.
- Implementation: `lam_confirm_e6.py` (CPU, after stage 3) copies the functions verbatim. **Self-test:** from the E4b/E5b files it reproduces the e6_lambda Qwen2.5-0.5B results:
  - U1 removes 0.984 [0.881, 1.054] of the λ = 1 loss.
  - U1 − λ0.7 = −0.86 pp [−1.74, −0.24].
  - M3 AUROC = 0.929 [0.840, 0.986].
  - Exact λ ties go to the smaller λ; this is made explicit by rounding the agreement scores to 12 decimals.
- **U1**: choose λ in the grid that maximises the mean, over the pair's two tasks, of merged-vs-single-adapter prediction agreement (argmax class; Spearman for STS-B) on **unlabeled** calibration inputs.
  - Calibration set: min(200, ⌊n_eval/2⌋) eval examples per task, drawn with `default_rng(20260927 + task index)`, disjoint from the scored examples. Labels are never used.
- Scoring on the eval examples outside calibration: D_test(λ). Reference λ_sel = held-out-selected λ (tuned TA, E4b grid). Regret = 100·(D_test(λ_rule) − D_test(λ_sel)) pp.
- Data: E6 cross-task pairs **R0 + S1 + P** pooled (S2 excluded). **Primary scope: G4 = {0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 1.0}, τ = 0.05.**
  CIs: task-block bootstrap 2,000, weights c_a·c_b, frozen seed rule (20260927 + Σ ord(scope key)).
- **H-U1**: SUPPORTED iff the 95% CI upper bounds of mean[regret(U1) − regret(λ = 0.7)] **and** mean[regret(U1) − regret(λ = 1.0)] are both < 0.
- **H-U1b**: SUPPORTED iff U1 removes ≥ 80% of the avoidable loss of λ = 1, i.e. 1 − mean regret(U1) / mean regret(λ = 1) ≥ 0.80 (point estimate; CI reported).
  **NOT APPLICABLE** if mean regret(λ = 1) ≤ 0, because the ratio is then undefined and there is no avoidable loss. This guard is not in e6_lambda (there, λ = 1 regret was always positive). It was added before any E6 merge, after the smoke test showed a spurious ratio with a non-positive denominator.
- **H-M3**: D̂_U = 1 − max_λ∈G4 agreement(λ); y = 1[D_test(λ_sel) > 0.05]. SUPPORTED iff AUROC ≥ 0.75 **and** CI lower bound > 0.5.
- **H-M3acc** (accuracy): decide "harmful merge" iff D̂_U ≥ **0.1174999999999999**.
  - The threshold is **frozen**: the accuracy-maximising cut on the 780 e6_lambda pairs (in-sample accuracy 0.879, prevalence 0.247), computed before any E6 merge.
  - SUPPORTED iff the CI lower bound of accuracy(M3) − accuracy(always merge) > 0.
  - Sensitivity: τ 0.02 → 0.0504428860721516; τ 0.10 → 0.2375.
- Secondary: per population; G7 (U1 and λ_sel over the 7-point grid); L-cal (labeled calibration) reference; eval-optimal floor; unfitted D̂_U > τ; recall/precision.
  The four hypotheses are reported separately, without multiplicity correction. If R0 or S1 is not completed (cap), the primary scope uses the completed populations, and this is stated.
